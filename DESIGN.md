# Nexmed design system

Owner: brand lane. Source of truth for tokens: `src/app/globals.css`. 3D source of truth: `blender/*.py`.

## Thesis

Nexmed is used by people who just got a hard diagnosis, by the families who organize around it, and by the researchers and companies who could help. The interface has to feel **calm, bright and trustworthy** — closer to a well-made botanical plate than to a sci-fi dashboard. The brand comes from the logo: a white DNA helix rising out of a forest inside a cyanotype-blue circle. Everything else follows from three ideas:

1. **Light, always.** White paper, logo blues, no dark sections, no neon, no gradient text.
2. **Evidence is visible.** Line style and colour carry meaning (observed / inferred / AI-extracted / community draft), never decoration.
3. **3D where it means something.** The helix that becomes a graph, the guide that listens, the glyph that tells you what a node is. Never 3D for its own sake, never without a still fallback.

## Naming

- **Nexmed** = the product (every UI string). **Nedamex** = the company (footer "Nexmed is a product of Nedamex", legal line, metadata `publisher`).
- Both live in `src/lib/site.ts` (`site.name`, `site.company`). Never hardcode either.

## Evidence kinds (the one visual grammar everyone shares)

| Kind | Meaning | Line | Class |
|---|---|---|---|
| observed | a source states it | solid `--brand-deep` | `.kind-observed` |
| inferred | Nexmed analysis (score + basis) | dashed `--brand` | `.kind-inferred` |
| extracted | OpenAI pulled it from a cited paper — needs expert review | dotted `--brand` | `.kind-extracted` |
| proposed | community draft — never evidence | ghost dashed `--line` | `.kind-proposed` |

`<NodeOrb kind="…">` renders the matching orb (ring style follows the line style).

## Typography

| Role | Face | Token |
|---|---|---|
| Body, UI | **Atkinson Hyperlegible** (Braille Institute; many rare diseases affect sight) — 400/700 | `--font-sans` |
| Display (h1–h2, numbers) | **Fraunces** (optical size axis) | `--font-display` / `.display` / `.serif` |
| Codes (ORPHA, HP, NCT, PMID) | **Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono** | `--font-mono` / `.mono` |

Loaded with `next/font/google` in `src/app/layout.tsx` (self-hosted at build, no runtime requests).

Scale (rem, root 16 px × `prefs.textScale` 1 / 1.15 / 1.3): display 4.5 / 3.75 / 2.6 (h1 lg / sm / base) · h2 2.25 → 1.875 · h3 1.125 · body 1–1.25 · small 0.875 · caption 0.75. Line length ≤ 70ch for body. `.eyebrow` = 0.75 rem, 0.14 em tracking, uppercase, bold, `--ink-3`.

## Space, shape, depth

- 4 px base. Section padding 64 px (py-16), card padding 20–32 px, gutters 16 px mobile / 24 px desktop, max width 72 rem (`max-w-6xl`).
- Radius `--radius` 14 px for cards; pills (999 px) for chips and buttons.
- Depth: one shadow, `--shadow-soft`. Borders do most of the work.

## Motion

- Tokens: `src/lib/motion.ts` (durations 80–1000 ms, `smooth` easing, springs). CSS loops: `.pulse`, `.flow-dash`, `.float-y`, `.helix-spin` — all inside `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.
- `[data-motion="reduce"]` (in-app pref) and the OS setting stop every CSS animation and transition app-wide.
- Motion explains state (the guide leans in while listening, a pulse climbs the graph). No parallax scroll-jacking, no autoplaying video.

## 3D rules

**When:** only where depth carries meaning — the hero (helix → graph), the voice guide (state you can see), graph glyphs (type at a glance). Everything else is flat.

**How:** every asset is generated by a reproducible headless Blender script, exported to glTF with Blender-authored animation clips:

| Asset | Script | Output | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero (helix from the logo forest into the graph) | `blender/build_hero.py` | `public/models/nexmed-hero.glb` (Draco, clips `Intro`, `Idle`) + `nexmed-hero.png` poster | 44k tris · 285 KB |
| Voice agent (pearl guide, helix halo, atlas orbits, voice petals) | `blender/build_agent.py` | `public/models/nexmed-agent.glb` (Draco, clips `Appear`, `Idle`, `Listen`, `Think`, `Speak`) + poster | 17k tris · 122 KB |
| Graph glyphs (one per entity type) | `blender/build_glyphs.py` | `public/models/nexmed-glyphs.glb` (geometry only) + contact sheet | 6.4k tris total · 124 KB |

Run: `"C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.2\blender.exe" -b --factory-startup -P blender/build_hero.py` (add `-- --no-render` to skip the Cycles poster, `-- --preview <png>` for a fast check). Shared helpers in `blender/nexmed_lib.py` (palette, curve tubes, NLA tracks, GLB post-pass that fixes the rest pose Blender bakes at the first animated frame).

**Budgets:** ≤ 60k triangles per scene · GLB < 2 MB (hero) / < 512 KB (others) · DPR ≤ 1.75 · `flat` (no tone mapping, so blues match the CSS) · Draco decoder self-hosted in `public/draco/` · three.js always code-split (`next/dynamic`, `ssr: false`).

**Lifecycle:** canvases stop rendering off-screen and in hidden tabs (`Scene3D`); the agent stops after its dismiss clip. Hero: the Cycles poster is the LCP and the 3D cross-fades in at the exact same camera, so the swap is invisible.

**Fallbacks (mandatory):** `useCan3D()` returns `"static"` for OS reduced motion, the in-app reduce-motion pref, no WebGL, < 4 cores, < 4 GB memory or Save-Data. Static = the Blender poster PNG (hero, agent) or the CSS orb. `NodeOrb` and `HelixLoader` are CSS 3D (no WebGL context) so they can appear many times per page.

**Clip layering (agent):** each clip drives its own layer of the hierarchy — `Appear` → `POP_*`, `Listen`/`Think`/`Speak` → `STATE_*` and `PETAL_*`, `Idle` → leaves — so states cross-fade without fighting. Live audio level multiplies the petals and core on top.

Shared components (`src/components/three/`): `Scene3D`, `Hero3D`, `AgentOrb`, `NodeOrb`, `HelixLoader`, `glyphs.ts` (`loadGlyphGeometries`, `useGlyphGeometries`), `useCan3D`, `palette.ts`.

## Accessibility

- WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (`--brand-deep` and darker for text on white; `--brand` only for large text and graphics).
- Focus is always visible: 2 px `--focus` outline, 2 px offset, on every interactive element. "Skip to content" link first in the tab order.
- Text scales to 130 % without loss (`prefs.textScale`); layouts tested at 390 px and 1440 px.
- 3D canvases are `aria-hidden` unless labelled; every 3D element has a text equivalent nearby (alt on posters, captions in the guide preview, `aria-live` for state).
- Colour is never the only signal: evidence kinds also differ by line style; states also differ by label.
- "Not medical advice" on every surface that renders answers.

## Banned

Dark backgrounds or dark sections · neon / glow-heavy UI · gradient text · amber for anything but gaps · invented numbers, testimonials, partner logos, team members or quotes · stock photos of patients · autoplaying audio or video · 3D without a still fallback · uncited claims in UI copy (product facts only; market facts with a visible source label, e.g. "Source: Hack-Nation Challenge 05 brief").
