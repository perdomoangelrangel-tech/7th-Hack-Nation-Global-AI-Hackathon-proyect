## Shot list

1. **Solo intro** for each member: same background, same framing, same height (shoot all of them in the same spot, one after another).
2. **Hook**: [Name 1] solo, slightly tighter framing.
3. **Laptop insert**: phone behind the shoulder, screen showing `/atlas` with Dravet (lower the screen brightness to avoid flicker; lock focus on the screen).
4. **Group wide shot**: everyone in frame, 2–3 takes of the closing line.
5. **B-roll (optional, 3–5 s each)**: hands on keyboard, whiteboard with the transit map sketch, sticky notes, coffee at 3 a.m. Real moments only.
6. **Room tone**: 10 s of silence in each location (for the editor to patch gaps).

## Phone recording tips

**Light**
- Face a window or a lamp, with the light in front of you and slightly to the side. Never stand with a window behind you.
- Turn off mixed colored lights. One soft source is better than many.
- Tap and hold on your face to lock exposure and focus (AE/AF lock).

**Audio**
- A quiet room with soft surfaces (curtains, sofa). Avoid kitchens and empty rooms.
- Phone 30–50 cm from the speaker, or use wired earbuds / a lav mic clipped to the shirt.
- Turn off fans and AC, and put the phone in airplane mode (no buzzes).
- Do a 5 s test and listen back with headphones before the real takes.

**Framing**
- **Landscape (16:9)**, 1080p at 30 fps, with the main (1×) lens, not the selfie camera if someone else can hold the phone.
- Camera at eye level, use a tripod or stack of books, and leave a little headroom. Eyes on the upper third line.
- Plain background with some depth (1–2 m behind you). A printed Nedamex transit line or the logo on a laptop works as a subtle prop.
- Look at the lens, not the screen.
- Clean the lens with your shirt. It really matters.

**Delivery**
- Short sentences, smile at the start, pause one beat before and after your line (it makes editing easy).
- Do 2–3 takes. Say your name exactly the same way each time.
- Wear solid colors: no small stripes, no logos.

**Files**
- Name the files `team-<name>-<take>.mp4` and drop them in a shared folder. The editor assembles them in CapCut, iMovie or DaVinci, with `out/TitleCard.mp4` at the start and `out/EndCard.mp4` at the end.
- Subtitles: burn in English captions (CapCut auto-captions), then check names and the numbers.

## Rules (same as the product)

- Only true facts about the team. The only number is "4.7 years" (EURORDIS Rare Barometer); say it as written.
- No patient stories or names unless they are real and have consent. The hook talks about "a family" in general.
- No medical claims ("cures", "diagnoses"). We map evidence; we're not giving medical advice.

## Resumen en español

**Objetivo:** un video de 60–90 s, grabado con el celular, que muestre quiénes somos y por qué construimos Nedamex.
**Estructura:** apertura con `TitleCard.mp4` (4 s) → gancho con el dato de 4.7 años (EURORDIS) → cada integrante dice "Soy [Nombre], [Rol]. Construí [parte]" (5–7 s cada uno) → qué hicimos (mapa de evidencia con 7 bases abiertas, agentes de voz que nunca inventan) → por qué nosotros (un dato real del equipo) → cierre grupal: "Rare disease, mapped. Every answer traced to its source." → `EndCard.mp4`.
**Luz:** de frente a una ventana o lámpara, nunca a contraluz; fija exposición y enfoque tocando la cara.
**Audio:** cuarto silencioso con telas, el celular a 30–50 cm o con audífonos con micrófono, ventiladores apagados, modo avión; prueba de 5 s con audífonos.
**Encuadre:** horizontal 16:9, 1080p y 30 fps, cámara a la altura de los ojos, fondo sencillo con algo de profundidad, mirar al lente y limpiar el lente.
**Reglas:** solo datos reales del equipo, ninguna historia de pacientes inventada, ninguna promesa médica. Nombres de archivo: `team-<nombre>-<toma>.mp4`.
